Service history

Rent billed = daily rent on deployed vehicles recorded in this app (opening balances before the start date are not included). Collected = rental + maintenance receipts.

Daily sales report (with payments received)

Excel has three sheets: Summary (per day), Daily Sales (one row per deployed vehicle with rent billed, and the rider’s payment received that day) and Payments received (every receipt). Covers days recorded in this app, after the opening date; earlier days are in your workbook.

Bulk payment upload

Upload an Excel or CSV file with columns Date, VRP ID, Amount, Head, Mode, Reference, Note. Head: Rental / Maintenance / Security deposit / Other (blank = Rental). Mode: Cash / UPI / Bank. If VRP ID is blank, a Mobile or exact Name column is used. Dates must be after the opening date. Rows with problems and duplicates are skipped.

Onboarding pipeline

Your access

Everyone signs in with email. New accounts start as Viewer (a rider: sees only their own wallet). The owner promotes staff in Supabase (SQL editor): Contributor records allotments, returns, payments, exchanges, calls, documents, service and rider details; Editor also edits or deletes payments, posts wallet adjustments and changes opening data.
